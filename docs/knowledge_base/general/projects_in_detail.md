# Yabibal's projects in detail (from the project code, November 2026)

## Eset Coffee (client work)
A three-app system for Eset Coffee, an Ethiopian specialty coffee brand. A public website in four languages (English, Arabic, Chinese, Amharic) presents coffees, origins, quality, export and a journal, and takes buyer enquiries and newsletter sign-ups. A staff admin manages content, the menu, enquiries, email campaigns and subscribers, with roles, a permission list, an approval queue and an audit log. Cafe guests scan a QR code to answer a feedback questionnaire, which staff review by location. One Fastify API alone holds the database, email and upload credentials; the website and admin call it server to server, and every operation is registered as public or staff-only. Pages are cached and refreshed only when staff change the content. Stack: Next.js 16, Fastify 5, TypeORM, PostgreSQL, Tailwind CSS v4, Zod, pnpm workspaces.

## Dual-Agent Architecture AI (own project)
A deliberation tool for architecture decisions. Two AI advisors, one on Claude and one on OpenAI, argue a design question over several rounds, and an arbiter returns a recommendation with a confidence score. The verdict includes consensus and dissenting points, key risks, implementation notes, a scorecard across eight engineering principles, generated system-architecture and sequence diagrams, and the token count and cost of each round. The debate runs as an n8n workflow; a React app starts it and follows progress through Supabase. Stack: React, TypeScript, Vite, n8n, Claude, OpenAI, Supabase, Mermaid.

## BillShare (own product)
A house bill-splitting app. Housemates share a room with real-time chat, split purchases item by item, and see balances that are never stored: they are recomputed from purchases and payments. Members settle at any time by paying through Telebirr and uploading a screenshot that the backend verifies. It has spending insights, an assistant that answers from the room's real balances and history, and an admin app. The FastAPI backend owns balance derivation, the Telebirr integration and the cycle-close job. Stack: Flutter, FastAPI, Supabase.

## Compliance Navigator (own project, in progress)
Graph-based decision support for Ethiopian business compliance and public services. Requirements are modelled as a dependency graph in Neo4j, and a roadmap engine turns a goal (for example a passport, a TIN or a trade licence) and a sub-city into ordered steps, each with the office, fee, documents, legal basis and whether it can be done at a MESOB one-stop centre. It mirrors the MESOB service catalogue (24 institutions, about 470 services in English and Amharic, 19 centres) into the graph and embeds it for retrieval. An assistant in English and Amharic, with voice, extracts the goal, builds a roadmap and cites its references; the model provider can be Ollama, Claude or an offline keyword mode. Guday Geday is a registry of licensed errand agents with ID verification, a public directory, client requests and ratings. A staff app covers metrics, data-source runs, a graph viewer and editor, and users and roles. Stack: FastAPI, Neo4j, PostgreSQL with pgvector, Next.js.

## Context Orchestrator (own open-source tool)
A local MCP server that gives AI coding assistants small, relevant slices of code instead of whole files, to cut API cost and context drift. It indexes a project locally using syntax-tree parsing, language-server lookups and a dependency graph, caches context, exposes six MCP tools, and ships a CLI for setup, indexing, metrics and benchmarking token savings. Stack: Python, MCP.

## Pest Leads Pro (client work, March to August 2025)
Dynamic SEO feeding a lead marketplace for a US company. One page template becomes a marketing page for every country, city and pest, filled from the database; n8n agents generate that data and validate it. A visitor's enquiry is sold to a vendor, the price is taken from the vendor's wallet, the visitor is redirected and the vendor notified. If the vendor does not make contact the lead is redirected to another vendor, and a bad lead is refunded. Scheduled agents handle SEO, performance, audit and reporting, and a digital PR agent turns pest-incident spikes into press pages and outreach.

## Feedlane (own product, founder)
Feedlane helps a business know what its customers really think: branded feedback forms, QR codes, AI insights and response management. A business creates a form from a category template, from its own paper questionnaire uploaded as a PDF, or from a description in English or Amharic; tests it; shares it by link, branded QR code, print template or embed; and works the responses in an inbox with status, assignment, alerts, AI categories and summaries, reports and export. It is built first for hotels, guesthouses, restaurants and cafes in Addis Ababa. The business chooses where its data lives: the cloud, a webhook to its own API, its own database, or self-hosted. It has three front ends (business dashboard, staff admin, public form) on a FastAPI backend. For pricing or a pilot, contact Yabibal directly.

## CourseBox (own open-source tool)
CourseBox turns a downloaded course zip into a Coursera-style learning space. Drop in an MIT OpenCourseWare export, a Udemy-style download or any folder of videos, PDFs and notes; it works out the structure (units, sessions, lectures, problem sets paired with solutions) and gives a course player with progress tracking. The zip is unpacked in a Web Worker and stored in the browser, so reading needs no account and no server. It has viewers for PDF, video with captions, Markdown, code and Jupyter notebooks. An optional free account saves courses and syncs progress across devices. Source: https://github.com/Yab112/coursebox
